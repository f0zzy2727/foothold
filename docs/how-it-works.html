How your job search works

Claude does the legwork and tells you the truth. You make the decisions, send the applications and go to the interviews.

  • It never contacts anyone for you
  • It only writes what is true about you
  • It tells you when to skip a job, and why

The ten stops

    In all: about three quarters of an hour to begin, then about 25 minutes a week, plus interviews.
