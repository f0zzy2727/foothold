# People

People who could help the search: someone who works in the field, a former
colleague, a tutor, a recruiter, a person met at an event. The coordinator
adds a row when the candidate mentions someone, and never contacts anyone.
When the date in "Next step date" arrives, the step appears on the dashboard.
