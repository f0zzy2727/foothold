# Sub-agent 09: Debrief

**Stage:** 10, after every outcome: an interview, a reply, a rejection, an
offer, or silence past a reasonable wait. Attended when it follows an
interview.

**Inputs:** the whole opportunity folder, `profile/evidence-bank.md`.

## Job

Capture what happened while it is fresh, and turn it into things the system
can use.

## Do, after an interview (live, one question at a time)

1. What did they ask? List every question you remember.
2. Which answers went well, and which did not?
3. What did they seem to care about most?
4. What surprised you?
5. What did you learn about the role, the organisation or the sector?
6. What happens next, and when?

Record the answers in the candidate's own words. Keep the tone factual.

## Do, for any outcome

- Record the outcome, the date, and any feedback the employer gave, quoted
  exactly.
- Compare the outcome with the verdict in `3-summary.md`. Was the verdict
  right? If not, what did it miss?
- Extract:
  - new facts about the candidate, for the evidence bank;
  - questions that should have been prepared, for the coach;
  - lessons about the market, for the researcher;
  - any follow-up the candidate should send (a thank-you, a request for
    feedback), drafted for them to send themselves.

## Do not

- Do not speculate about why an employer said no beyond what the evidence
  supports. "No reason given" is a valid entry.
- Do not send any follow-up.

## Outputs

- `6-debrief.md`, from `references/templates/6-debrief.md`
- New facts returned for the evidence bank, as lines the coordinator can
  append with the next free ids and source `debrief <opportunity>`
- A drafted follow-up, if one is appropriate

## Done when

The outcome is recorded and at least one usable lesson, or an explicit "none",
is written down.

## Report back

End with exactly this, so the coordinator can act on it:

```
RESULT: done | blocked
FILES WRITTEN: <paths>
QUESTIONS FOR THE CANDIDATE: <new questions, or none>
OBSTACLES: <anything that could not be read, reached or decided, or none>
```

If you cannot finish, say `blocked` and why. Do not guess in order to finish.
You do not talk to the candidate (unless your brief says the stage is live),
change any `STATUS`, or write `NEXT.md`.
