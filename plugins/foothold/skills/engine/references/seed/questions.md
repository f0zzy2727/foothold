# Questions

One queue for the whole search. Only the first unanswered question is shown to
the candidate. They answer in chat, or here on the line beginning `A:`.
Answers are copied into `profile/evidence-bank.md` on the next run, and the
question is marked `[answered <date>]`.

Each entry takes two lines: the question, starting with its number and the
opportunities it affects in square brackets, then a line beginning `A:`.
