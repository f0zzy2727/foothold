# Disclaimer

Foothold is a tool that helps a person run their own job search with Claude.
Please read this before relying on it.

## What it is not

- **Not professional advice.** Nothing it produces is careers, legal,
  financial, tax or immigration advice. For contracts, visas, employment
  rights and pay negotiations, ask a qualified person.
- **No promise of results.** Nobody can guarantee an interview, an offer or
  a job. A "Strong fit" verdict is a judgement, not a prediction.
- **Not a recruiter or an agent.** It never applies, emails or contacts
  anyone for you. You send everything yourself.

## What can go wrong

- **AI makes mistakes.** Verdicts, research, pay figures and summaries of
  adverts can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Market figures are
  estimates. Check anything that matters against the original source.
- **You are responsible for what you send.** Read every CV, covering note and
  message before it goes out under your name. The built-in checks reduce the
  chance of an inaccurate claim. They cannot remove it, and they cannot tell
  whether something you told the tool is true.
- **Adverts are not verified.** The tool cannot confirm that an advert or an
  employer is genuine. Be careful with any role that asks for money, bank
  details or identity documents before an offer, however the tool rated it.
- **Early software.** This is a pilot release. Parts of it have been tested
  only by its author. The README says which.

## Using it for someone else

The search works only if the person looking for work owns it. Set it up with
them, not for them. They need their own Claude account and must meet
Anthropic's terms for having one.
